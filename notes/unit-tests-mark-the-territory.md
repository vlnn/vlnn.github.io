Unit tests are useful during iterative development, but once a project enters maintenance they become a bit of a burden — which is also one of their functions: [marking territory](https://yosefk.com/blog/unit-tests-mark-territory-more-than-squash-bugs.html). The developer pins down their understanding of the system, and the tests slow down anyone else's changes by explaining how (and sometimes why) things are the way they are.
