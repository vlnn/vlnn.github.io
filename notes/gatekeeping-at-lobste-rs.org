Several years https://lobste.rs/ were my favourite source of professional truth: it's a link aggregator with some attitude, quite transparent in how they operate, and just good overall! There were several dramas, but not something that you don't expect from site that is built on user-provided content.

It all started with geo/VPN blocking (I guess they started with [[https://lobste.rs/s/ukosa1/uk_users_lobsters_needs_your_help_with][geoblock of UK]] due to some crazy Ofcom regulations, then blocking Brave browser users (due to [[https://lobste.rs/s/iopw1d/what_s_up_with_lobste_rs_blocking_brave]["years-long pattern of repeated bad actions followed by dismissive statements with no countervailing behaviors]]"). Well, while not being fun, I can relate.

But now they are removing the links to the sites that are created with AI:

[[file:static/lobsters.png]]

It's really hard to draw a line between "=Ad, slop=" and 99% of modern sites, this is disturbing as hell. But gatekeeping things due to ~"They didn’t even bother to change the default Claude theme."~ is even crazier to me.

To be honest, I think that gatekeeping can be rightful, but can't think of any other cases except the open war. Are we at war with AI already?
