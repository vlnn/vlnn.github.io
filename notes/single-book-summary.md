Strategy of self-study via creating and maintaining the single knowledge book holding all the learning material and insights (opposed to usual many sources across books and notebooks and similar to [Forster's lists](forster-lists.md) that summarize everything into the [single source of truth](single-source-of-truth.md)). This is done either from scratch or taking as a base one of existing books. It's not the encyclopedia though, rather the heavily-filtered list of things student wants to remember (they may even remove things they know well). Review process is usually based on [8-4-2-1](8-4-2-1-method.md) [method of spaced repetition](method-of-spaced-repetition.md)**.** 

This makes reviewing much faster and more productive due to constant re-reviewing   same structure with just a small changes and additions. 

Single book frequently contains both the "imported" materials (from lectures, other books) and "[behavioral rules](behavioral-rules.md)" (notes on how to approach some of the problems, what usual errors are to check the answers for, etc created basing on [feedback](feedback.md))
