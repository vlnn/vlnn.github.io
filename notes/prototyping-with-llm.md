When prototyping software, lots of time goes into the creation of prototypes; [more prototypes the better](more-prototypes-the-better.md), as they provide better quality of feedback. LLMs improving the prototyping cycle generating code much faster than any human can; quality of the code is not that important when prototyping the interfaces; quality of interfaces are not that important when experimenting with the architecture.

One day of experiments took me to create [vlnn/kobold](https://github.com/vlnn/kobold) that a) can be already used, b) is useful to me, c) enables me more ideas on how similar interfaces could look like. If I did everything by myself, it would took me months of experiments and days of struggle. Days of stuggle are common denominator though.
