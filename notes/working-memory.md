The cognitive system with a limited capacity but quick response: classic 7±2 (later 4) items that humans can [chunk bigger quantity of information](chunking.md)  into and switch between.

Working memory is limited, and its capacity is roughly fixed at birth. 

While higher working-memory capacity is related to higher general intelligence, working-memory capacity is not the be-all and end-all for performance. Higher capacity enables faster learning, but our unlimited long-term memory removes limitations on how much we could ultimately learn in total.

Relates to [cognitive load](cognitive-load.md).
