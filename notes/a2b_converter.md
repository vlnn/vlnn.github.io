[a2b tool](https://github.com/vlnn/andy2brain) is a /private/ tool to convert several [Digital Gardens](digital-garden) into [TheBrain](https://thebrain.com) **brz* archives and back. 

I've used the tool to backup [Andy Matuschak](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/) into local brain so I can search it without internets. And then I decided to also use it to help myself with writing and interlinking notes for this site.
