### Applications
* [Endel](https://endel.io/)

Feedback  for generative music in those apps is time/pulse/GPS coordinates etc. Not related to the [generators](generator.md) in radio electronics sense, as they actually are using existing state of the environment as their [Negative feedback](feedback.md).
