The change in heritable characteristics over successive generations. **[Natural selection](natural-selection.md)** is the main mechanism that drives evolution. But in the biology there are lots of others mechanisms:<br>
* **Genetic drift** — random changes in allele frequencies, especially powerful in small populations (a storm kills half a population regardless of [fitness](fitness.md))
* **Gene flow** — migration moving alleles between populations
* **Mutation** — the ultimate source of new variation, and a (weak) directional force itself
* **Sexual selection** — often treated as a subtype of [natural selection](natural-selection.md), where the ["environment" doing the selecting ](environment-selection.md)is mate preference (peacock tails)
