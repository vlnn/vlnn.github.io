This is widely used (and usually mismeasured or not measured at all) improvement rate that everyone kinda understand but rarely see (see [10x engineers](10x-engineer.md)). 

Realistic scenarios: 
* [algorithmic complexity ](algorithmic-complexity.md)fixes O(n²) → O(n log n)
* [memoization](memoization.md)
* Changing [big batch to streaming](switching-from-batch-to-streaming.md)
* eliminating useless steps (second best [Chuck Moore](chuck-moore.md)'s technique: first is not implementing them)
* administrative decisions: simplier processes, proper reviews etc.
