Andy Matuschak introduces [Evergreen notes](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/z5E5QawiXCMbtNtupvxeoEX) as a specific format of knowledge work (and also as fundamental unit of such work).

"Evergreenness" of those notes is never explained, but my current understanding is that they have to be abstract enough to be referred to in different contexts.
