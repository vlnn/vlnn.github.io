---
title: Creativity Is only Possible in the Open Mode
date: 2026-08-29
brain-id: d11326a6-d44c-4cd2-b717-092dc39e63b0
---

The [closed mode](closed-mode-of-operating.md) is structurally hostile to new ideas. It provides anxiousness and urgentness, and anxiety narrows attention. You want the answer now, so you grab the first plausible one. 

New ideas, by contrast, are usually half-formed, slightly absurd, and don't immediately look useful. A mind under pressure discards them before they've had a chance to develop. So the closed mode isn't just less creative, it actively filters out the raw material [creativity](creative-persons.md) works with.
