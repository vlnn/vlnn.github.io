Operant conditioning is learning through the [feedback](feedback.md): a [behavior](behavior.md) is followed by an outcome, and that outcome changes behavioral probability.
* Followed by something good (reinforcement) → behavior becomes more frequent. A rat presses a lever, gets food, presses more.
* Followed by something bad (punishment) → behavior becomes less frequent. Touch a hot stove, get burned, stop touching stoves.

*There are lots of other learning mechanisms: Non-associative learning, Observational learning, Cognitive/latent learning etc. Behaviorism claimed operant conditioning as the only one though.*
