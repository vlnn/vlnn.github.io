Minimalist presentation style created by Japanese programmer Masayoshi Takahashi in the early 2000s. It's designed to keep audiences engaged and focused on the speaker rather than dense slides.

The method is built on a simple insight: ***when slides contain too much information, audiences read instead of listen.*** By reducing each slide to a single emphatic phrase or word, the audience's attention stays on the presenter.

1. Write big
2. Write less
3. Many slides is OK
4. Text only
5. Speaker matters
