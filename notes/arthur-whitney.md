designer of [A+](https://aplwiki.com/wiki/A%2B), the K family of languages, and Q, and cofounder of [Kx Systems](https://aplwiki.com/wiki/Kx_Systems). Whitney was a major figure in the development of [leading axis theory](https://aplwiki.com/wiki/Leading_axis_theory) and an influence on [J](https://aplwiki.com/wiki/J)'s early development. He is also known for his compact C [programming style](programming-styles.md) enabled by macros, sometimes called [Whitney C](https://aplwiki.com/wiki/Whitney_C).
