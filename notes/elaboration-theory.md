Elaboration theory proposes seven major strategy components: 
1. an [elaborative sequence,](elaborative-sequencing.md) 
2. learning prerequisite sequences,
3. summary, 
4. synthesis, 
5. analogies,
6. cognitive strategies
7. learner control.
