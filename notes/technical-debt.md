Technical debt in programming is [sloppy code](sloppy-code.md). In current [world of startups and venture capital](world-of-startups-and-venture-capital.md) it makes [perfect sense to move very fast and leave problems to future owners](move-fast-and-break-things.md). But sometimes it's just a [sloppy code](sloppy-code.md), that is hard to [read](reading-code.md) and [maintain](maintaining-legacy.md) due to misunderstanding of the problem and bad architectural choices —those usually go hand in hand, so the real problem is something more general. 

Good software should be easy to change because it is what differs [Software vs Hardware](software-vs-hardware.md).
