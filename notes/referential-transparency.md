title: Referential transparency

brain-id: 2ec0f851-2bcd-4539-bd62-b1f8342dfd1f

Referential transparency is a property of an expression: it can be replaced by its value anywhere in the program without changing the program’s behaviour. Calls to pure functions (deterministic and free of side effects) are referentially transparent.
