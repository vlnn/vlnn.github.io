Eremitic life ({ Erˊe-mit′ic, Erˊe-mit′ic-al} Of or pertaining to an eremite; hermitical; living in solitude) in Catholicism has different layers:

1.  `Canon 603 hermits` or `diocesan hermits` are under their bishop's rulings and approvals. For example, [Sister Laurel O'Neal](https://notesfromstillsong.blogspot.com) is of that cohort.
2.  `Lay hermits` have no canonical standings. E.g. [Dr Toomas Karmo](Dr-Toomas-Karmo.md) with web representation at [Dr. Toomas Karmo](https://toomaskarmo.blogspot.com/2026/04/toomas-karmo-toravere-erakla-praegu-ja.html) and [his library and setup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jzKzLE3Z4VQ)
