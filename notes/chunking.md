Chunking is a technique commonly used by people to help them remember the information.

A chunk is a small set of items (4±1 in many studies) having a common, strong, association with each other (and a much weaker one to items in other chunks).

i.e. people recall the telephone numbers is optimal if numbers are grouped into chunks of up to three digits: 212-85-06
