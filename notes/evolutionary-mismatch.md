A population-level state of elevated chronic stress resulting from rapid environmental change. For example, humans experience evolutionary mismatch with the modern environment due to [anthropogenic factor](anthropogenic-factor.md)s, encountering novel stressors that evolution cannot adapt to quickly enough (the [pace of change](pace-of-change.md) exceeds [natural  selection](natural-selection.md), and cultural transmission overwhelms evolutionary pressure). This is evidenced by rising rates of morbid obesity, type 2 diabetes, and other metabolic disorders.
