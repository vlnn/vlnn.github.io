Adaptive music is [generative music](generative-music.md) where feedback is coming in realtime (as opposed to pre-recorded or planned).

A naive way to adapt music to the gameplay is to make separate music themes for each level. But not unlike the theme of Darth Vader in Star Wars movies, it was introduced to games as well —and the more complex and non-linear the gameplay is, the harder to do it in style. Imagine how music of Star Wars game would look like if by some reason Darth Vader starting entering/exiting the room, thus playing his theme and then canceling it every time. 

### Games
* TIE Fighter
* Space Invaders Extreme (every piu-piu shot sound is actually played not when player fires, but when OST has it's beat, so you always shoot in the beat).

### Live music
All live music is adaptive, as musicians are constantly adapting their sound to the environment (other performers, the area, auditory reaction, internal urges).
