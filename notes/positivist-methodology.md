Generally, a research approach based on the belief that an [objective reality](objective-reality.md) exists and can be studied through observable, measurable facts.

 It applies the  [scientific method](scientific-method.md) to social phenomena, prioritizing quantitative data, hypothesis testing, and statistical analysis to find general laws.

In [behaviorism](behaviorism.md) context: studying only observable [Stimuli](stimuli.md) and [behavior](behavior.md)
