[Programming is hard](programming-is-hard.md) and thus there is no single development, in either technology or management technique, that will by itself promise even a tenfold improvement in software productivity, reliability, or simplicity within a decade. (as of 1986)

Brooks evaluated the hyped "bullets" of his day — Ada,[ object-oriented programming](smalltalk-oop-is-not-mainstream-oop.md), [AI](ai.md), expert systems, automatic programming, graphical programming, program verification, better workstations — and predicted none would deliver [10x improvement](10x-improvement.md) alone.

Also introduced into wide audience the [Essential complexity](essential-complexity.md) and [Accidental complexity](accidental-complexity.md) dichotomy.
