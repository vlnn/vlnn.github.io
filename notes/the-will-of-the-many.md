Roman-like social (highly structured, hierarchical from the bottom to the top) and political adventure with scifi distopian flavour. Reminds me of some early "adventure" or "interactive fiction" games from ZX Spectrum I never finished: Snowball, Return to the Eden.

The protagonist **Vis** reminds me of **Myrddin Emrys** from [Mary Stewart](mary-stewart.md)'s Crystal Cave; but Emrys was 12 when he was got to the big adventure, and Vis is 18; it is fun how less realistic it makes Vis related to Myrddin, or dumber: he is pretty much young dumbhead who is also a revolutionary prince in the pseudo-Republic he hates, used by one of the three superpowers of the government (around first third of the book). 

The style is modern e.g. more dialogues, less landscapes. I wonder if it's a heaven for people who were skipping long nature descriptions in Tolkien's books and rushed to the action (I was one of them for sure).

Also some themes a bit deeper than usual modern fiction gets into: e.g. [political guilt](political-guilt.md), [collective responsibility](collective-responsibility.md) of people supporting and not supporting the state they are a part of, even a bit of external politics approaches.
