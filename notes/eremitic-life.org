Eremitic life ({ Erˊe-mit′ic, Erˊe-mit′ic-al} Of or pertaining to an eremite; hermitical; living in solitude) in Catholicism has different layers:

1. =Canon 603 hermits= or =diocesan hermits= are under their bishop's rulings and approvals. For example, [[https://notesfromstillsong.blogspot.com][Sister Laurel O'Neal]] is of that cohort.
2. =Lay hermits= have no canonical standings. E.g. [[file:Dr-Toomas-Karmo.org][Dr Toomas Karmo]] with web representation at [[https://toomaskarmo.blogspot.com/2026/04/toomas-karmo-toravere-erakla-praegu-ja.html][Dr. Toomas Karmo]] and [[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jzKzLE3Z4VQ][his library and setup]]
