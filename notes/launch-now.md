Great advice of limited applicability: you get your information fast (pretty much money or no money) fast, have more pivot options, get more of a market (more information than resources as [first rarely wins](first-rarely-wins.md)) -- but also you can flop great idea if it's not ready, which isn't *that* bad as you may think. Still, it can be pretty bad for niche products, where one bad start can circulate over 100s of professionals fast and stop the flow, or the ones where [iteration is expensive](iteration-can-be-expensive.md).
You [don't have to make the launch big](don-t-have-to-make-the-launch-big.md): it's enough to have a handful of users providing you real data.
