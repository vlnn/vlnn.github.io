---

Organisms actively choose where to live: a moth picking bark that matches its coloration, a bird choosing a nesting microclimate. 

By choosing environments, organisms partly choose their own selective pressures. This can shield populations from selection (buffering) or accelerate divergence (if different variants prefer different habitats, that's a fast route toward speciation).
