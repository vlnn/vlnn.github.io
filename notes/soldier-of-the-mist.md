Interesting! Protagonist is [unreliable narrator](unreliable-narrator.md) but his unreliability is actually lesser than you might think: 
1. Latro is Latin mercenary in Persian army with cultural difference so strong that  Athens for him is Thought (as literal translation) and Spartans are Rope-Makers). Even his own name is just "Mercenary" or "Soldier" — but that is my problem as an [unreliable reader ](unreliable-reader.md)I guess. Still, cultural differences are shown well.
2. he has brain damage that makes him forget roughly everything happened before he wakes, but this makes him to document all the stuff he experiences thus making him paradoxically more reliable

Seeing gods... Not unreliable, and looks more complex than being hallucinations. Very interesting.

It would be really hard to read the book without looking for the "real names" for the ones protagonist is using — which maybe should add constant tension to the book holding the reader in the partial mist due to some names analogies grokked and some not — but I just can't step back and hold that tension. There is a *A Chapter Guide to Gene Wolfe's Latro Novels* but it would add much more complexity to the reading process, even if relieving the tension of that mist of partial understanding.
