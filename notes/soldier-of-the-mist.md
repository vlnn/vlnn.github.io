Interesting! Protagonist is [unreliable narrator](unreliable-narrator.md) but his unreliability is twofold: 
1. he is Latin soldier in Persian army with cultural difference so strong that  Athens for him is Thought (as literal translation) and Spartans are Rope-Makers)
2. he has brain damage that makes him forget roughly everything happened before he wakes, but this makes him to document all the stuff he experiences thus making him paradoxically more reliable

Seeing gods... Not unreliable, and looks more complex than being hallucinations. Very interesting.
