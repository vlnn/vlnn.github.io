BLUF puts the most important thing first so a message could be understood in a single rapid reading.

A BLUF message has three parts, in descending order of importance:
* **On top goes usual bottom line:** the conclusion, decision, or request, stated in the first sentence or two
* **The reasons:** the context needed to accept or act on it
* **The details:** background and supporting data

So a reader who stops after the opening still knows what is being asked or claimed but can dig deeper if they needs to.
