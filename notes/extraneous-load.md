extraneous [cognitive load](cognitive-load.md) is working-memory effort to work with unnecessary information that contributes nothing to the goal, but is required or imposed by the task representation. It is similar to using wrong tool or syntax for a job and thus related to [Notation as a Tool of Thought](notation-as-a-tool-of-thought.md).

Extraneous load is generally higher for beginners because they cannot distinguish between intrinsic and extraneous information easily.
