Intelligence over some level (\~115 IQ) is "entry point" to the creativity. 

Factors of creativity are rather consistent:
* openness to experience (the strongest personality correlate), 
* tolerance for ambiguity, 
* intrinsic motivation,
* deep domain knowledge.
