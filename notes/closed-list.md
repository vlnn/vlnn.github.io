an ever-growing open todo list is demoralizing and unmanageable. ***Do not*** "work harder" or "get more disciplined"

Instead, you stop adding tasks into today: everything that arrives today goes to tomorrow's list, and tomorrow's list is closed — a finite "will do" list rather than an infinite "might do" list. 

Finishing your list daily becomes achievable and [diagnostic](commitment-audit-via-feedback.md).
