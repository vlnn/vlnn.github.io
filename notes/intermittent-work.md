Instead of starting and finishing a task in one go, engage with it in distributed sessions — treating it as an activity rather than a task. Sessions are open-ended: no minimum, work while it flows, stop without guilt. 

In practice each session tends to yield real progress; an item that's touched repeatedly without any is telling you something.

*"[little and often](little-and-often.md)"* is good for [knowledge work](knowledge-work.md), but not that good for [kanban-like work](demand-driven-work.md).
