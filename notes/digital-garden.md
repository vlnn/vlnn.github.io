Somehow Digital Garden term means today "shared interlinked personal notes online", even though it all started with 1998 [essay "Hypertext Gardens"](https://www.eastgate.com/garden/Enter.html) by Mark Bernstein, and it was pretty much meta on how to write hypertext sequentially with community in mind. Most useful is [Seven lessons from gardening](seven-lessons-from-gardening.md).



Nice stuff, but it's not "Personal wiki" or "All my life in the network" that now is being associated with the term "Digital Garden".
