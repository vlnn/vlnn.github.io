Notes without structure are good for several cases:
1. Temporarily expand [Working Memory](working-memory.md) (eg to find a structure)
2. Optionally Inline small piece of information into the bigger piece (you don't expect every footnote to consist of preface, chapters and the index -- it's just a tiny atomic description in case you need it)
