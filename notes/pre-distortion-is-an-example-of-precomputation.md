---
title: Pre-distortion is an example of precomputation.
date: 2026-08-15
brain-id: 0889505b-a546-4fbd-9e43-b42ac49576e8
---

CRT brightness responds nonlinearly to voltage. Instead of putting a correction circuit in every television, the camera signal was pre-distorted with the inverse curve (signal\^(1/γ)) at the studio. The distortions of camera-side encoding and CRT physics cancel out, and the viewer sees correct brightness. One correction at the source instead of millions in receivers.
