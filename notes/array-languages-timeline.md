Seventy years of same idea: [notation as a tool of thought](notation-as-a-tool-of-thought.md).

[Ken Iverson](ken-iverson.md) invented the notation it at Harvard while teaching under [Howard Aiken](howard-aiken.md), IBM turned it into programming language called APL, that became the ancestor to J, K, Q, BQN, Uiua .

## APL
* 1957 — [Iverson](ken-iverson.md) starts on his notation at Harvard as new math notation (no computers yet)
* 1962 — the book *A Programming Language* gives the notation its name
* 1963 — *Automatic Data Processing*, written with [Fred Brooks](fred-brooks.md), uses the notation too
* 1965 — IVSYS ("Iverson System"), the first implementation for IBM 7090 written in FORTRAN
* 1966-11-27 — the first APL\360 workspace is saved; this is the day APL counts as its birthday
* 1968 — APL\360 leaves IBM as a public release
* 1970 — APL\*PLUS: STSC and I.P. Sharp sell APL as a time-sharing service
* 1975-09 — the IBM 5100, a "portable" computer with APL in ROM
* 1980-08 — Iverson's Turing lecture, [Notation as a Tool of Thought](notation-as-a-tool-of-thought.md), is printed in CACM
* 1981 — NARS from STSC: the first APL with nested arrays; SHARP APL answers with boxes
* 1983 — Dyalog APL is released at the APL83 conference; Iverson's *Rationalized APL* introduces the rank operator
* 1984 — IBM ships APL2, the nested design Jim Brown began in 1971
* 1996 — [John Scholes](john-scholes.md) adds dfns to Dyalog, after studying Scheme
* 2013 — GNU APL
* 2014 — Co-dfns, an APL compiler written in APL
## J
* 1989 — [Arthur Whitney](arthur-whitney.md) writes a one-page interpreter in an afternoon: [the Incunabulum](whitney-s-incunabulum.md)
* 1989-08-27 — [Roger Hui](roger-hui.md) starts J from that page
* 1990-02 — first public demo of J, at the Toronto APL SIG; Eric Iverson founds Iverson Software the same month
* 1990-08 — J is released at APL90 in Copenhagen
* 2011-03 — the J source is opened under GPL3
## K and Q
* 1988 — [Whitney](arthur-whitney.md) extends his lean APL dialect A into A+ at Morgan Stanley
* 1993 — K; [Whitney](arthur-whitney.md) and Janet Lustgarten found Kx Systems, at first under an exclusive contract with UBS
* 1998 — the UBS contract ends and kdb, a database written in K, goes on sale
* 2003 — kdb+, the 64-bit kdb, arrives together with q
* 2018 — [Whitney](arthur-whitney.md) co-founds Shakti Software
## New wave
* 2020 — BQN: Marshall Lochbaum leaves Dyalog and turns his "fixed APL" into a personal project
* 2020 — KAP
* 2023 — Uiua by Kai Schmidt: stack-based and tacit, with glyphs inspired by BQN
## People
* 1960..1980 — Iverson at IBM
* 1979-10-29 — Iverson receives the Turing Award
* 1980..1987 — Iverson at I.P. Sharp Associates
* 1988..1993 — [Whitney](arthur-whitney.md) at Morgan Stanley
* 2004-10-19 — [Ken Iverson](ken-iverson.md) dies in Toronto
* 2019-02-18 — [John Scholes](john-scholes.md) dies
* 2021-10-16 — [Roger Hui](roger-hui.md) dies
