In the context of the **Attitude Note (태도정리노트)** and **Behavioral Guidelines (행동강령)**, a [behavioral](behavior.md) rule is a structured, action-oriented instruction designed to replace unpredictable "intuition" or "panic" under stress with highly automated, machine-like routines

|Trigger type|Trigger|Guideline it activates|:(table="column-widths:-1,0,256,0"):
|Somatic|Jaw clenches, breath goes shallow, heat in the chest|Pause, exhale once, count to three before speaking|
|Emotional|First flicker of defensiveness or irritation|Ask a question instead of making a statement|
|Verbal|Someone says "you always…" / "you never…"|Repeat their point back before responding|
|Situational|Slack notification arrives mid-deep-work|Do not open it; note the time and continue|
|Social|You get interrupted mid-sentence|Let them finish, then say "let me complete the thought"|
|Temporal|6 p.m., laptop still open|Write three lines in the Attitude Note, then close it|
|Impulse|Hand reaches for the phone without a reason|Stand up instead and drink water|
|Relational|You catch yourself rehearsing a rebuttal while they talk|Stop rehearsing, listen for one thing you agree with|

### Those rules can be just rules of thumb for any area:
#### **Math**
*Trigger:* Identifying a complex matrix problem on a test sheet 
-> 
*Action: “First action: Look for linear combinations immediately”* 

#### Law
*Trigger:* You are asked if a worker has a "duty to pay damages" due to violating a non-compete clause
->
*Action (Reverse Outline Steps): “To have a duty to pay damages* → *there must be a valid non-compete duty* → *which requires the non-compete agreement to be valid* → *which means it must not violate Article 103 of the Civil Act* → *Next Step: State the judgment criteria for Article 103.”*

or even better

*Trigger:* Doing self-study or taking a mock exam
->
*Action: “Always physically tear out the relevant legal code sheets and place them next to your notebook. Force yourself to find the exact article within 20 seconds before writing a single word of your explanation.”*
