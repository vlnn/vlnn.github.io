Source: [The Kluever Maneuver](https://jordi.inversethought.com/blog/the-kluever-maneuver/)

Fixing bugfix, do it in two commits.
1. Write a passing test that exemplifies the buggy behavior.
2. Fix the bug and also fix the test we just wrote.

This
* Clearly shows exactly what the bug is
* Proves  that the bugfix actually fixed the bug
* CI is green at every commit
