It's hard to rebuild the building, or even scale the building (imagine the hotel where owner could order several additional floors during the peak of the tourist season... you wish!). 

So "softness" of software should be a quality that differ it from "hard" hardware. What exactly is this quality? I guess the initial idea was that we have a hardware+software as a system, that is relatively adaptable long time after building with changes in software. You don't usually want to change the whole door or even house every time you lose your keys, but you rather go make new key:  this is a bit similar how software adapts hardware to changing world of people who lose their keys.

Interestingly, [Software being Everchanging](software-is-everchanging.md) is exactly why [Software Is Complex](software-is-complex.md). I guess there are [Different complexities](different-complexities.md)
