Feedback is the return to the input of a part of the output of a machine, system, or process.

* Controlled feedback is usually a negative feedback, that is bringing the system to a controlled, stable state; usually associated with [amplifiers](amplifier.md).
* Uncontrolled feedback is positive feedback, that increases itself, thus bringing more  and more changes to the system, making it [generative system](generator.md) *(guitar feedback in the music is a positive feedback between guitar, mic and monitor, generating hardly controlled sound of 70s rock music)*.
