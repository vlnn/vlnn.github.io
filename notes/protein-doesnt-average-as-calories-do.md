title: Protein doesn’t average (as calories do)
date: 2026-11-06
brain-id: 0ae1c7bc-5272-4187-bf99-851733bc920f
---

The averaging window for protein is much shorter than for calories: body fat is a great averaging buffer that is impossible to saturate.<br>

Protein has only free amino acids in the blood and tissues that is rather small buffer, with its level that can meaningfully change within hours, not days/weeks. That's why daily total of the protein is most important metric, but weekly consumption of calories is usually used for calorie deficit feedback.
