[Natural selection](natural-selection.md) is the *process*; fitness is the variable that describes and predicts it. 

"Selection favors trait X" is really "genotypes carrying X have higher **fitness** than alternatives." 

Selection, in population-genetic terms, is the existence of fitness differences between heritable variants. No fitness differences means no selection (only drift).
