1. **Context** — the system as a single box, surrounded by its users and the external systems it talks to. Answers "what is this and who/what interacts with it?"
2. **Container** — zooms into the system to show its deployable/runnable units: web app, API service, database, message broker, mobile app. Shows the major technology choices and communication paths — this is the level most people mean when they sketch "the architecture."
3. **Component** — zooms into one container to show its internal building blocks: modules, groups of related functionality, and their interactions.
4. **Code** — zooms into one component down to classes/functions, usually a UML class diagram.
