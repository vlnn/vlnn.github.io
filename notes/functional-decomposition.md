Way of solving a complex problem by breaking it into smaller sub-problems, solving each with a small single-purpose function, and composing those functions into the solution.
Decomposition continues until each function is trivial.
The resulting functions are often [reusable](reusable-programs.md) for other problems.
Creating [DSL](domain-specific-language.md) is an example of using functional decomposition.
