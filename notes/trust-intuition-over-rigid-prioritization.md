[Forster](mark-forster.md) argues that classical prioritization (A/B/C, urgent/important matrices) doesn't really work in practice. His systems rely on scanning a long list until something "stands out".

 He believes the [rational mind ](rational-mind.md)and [intuitive mind](intuitive-mind.md) should work together, and the standing-out mechanism is how you let the deeper mind vote.
