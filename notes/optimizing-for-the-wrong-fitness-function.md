In environments with [artificial selection](artificial-selection.md) (opposed to the [Natural Selection](natural-selection.md)), [Fitness](fitness.md) function is, well, artificial, and as such may enforce things different from success in reproduction. E.g. in normal office environment people don't expect they will be selected for their ability to reproduce, but rather for their intellectual abilities, as they should add to organizational survivability much more than just a quantity and quality of children worker will have.

One of the worst things manager/enforcer can do is start optimizing for the wrong fitness function, spending resources for things that will not be useful in one way or another in the future. In reality, fitness function in artificial environment is complex thing: imagine, for instance, how many contradictory goals high management has.
