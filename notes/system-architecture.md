System architecture is the high-level structure of a system: the set of components it's made of, the responsibilities of each component, and the ways they interact — plus the principles and constraints that shaped those choices.

It typically answers questions like:
* What are the major parts? (services, databases, queues, frontends, external integrations)
* How do they communicate? (synchronous APIs, message brokers, shared storage, events)
* Where does data live and how does it flow through the system?
* What are the cross-cutting decisions? (scalability strategy, fault tolerance, security boundaries, deployment model)

System Architecture can described by [C4 model](c4-model.md), bundles of Architectural Patterns and reasoning, or million other enterprise frameworks.
